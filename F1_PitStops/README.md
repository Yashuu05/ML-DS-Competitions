# *F1 Pit Stops Prediction Kaggle Swag Competition*

---

### ABOUT
- This is a *Kaggle's Swag* competition hosted and organized by kaggle in month of May 2026.
- The competiton challenges participants to use machine learning concepts and tools to predict the probabilities of target label.
- This is a classical *binary classification* problem where target label has eithr `1` or `0`.
- The performance and score is calculated on the basis of the `ROC_AUC_SCORE` of the trained model.  

---

### GOAL:

- Predict whether a Formula 1 driver will pit on the next lap.
